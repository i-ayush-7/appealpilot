FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is this legal or medical advice?

No. AppealPilot drafts documents that help you use the appeal rights you already have. You review every fact before anything is sent, and we recommend adding a letter from your doctor.

Which denials does it handle?

US health insurance claim denials for internal appeals: medical necessity, prior authorization, out-of-network and emergency care, experimental treatment, coding and benefit exclusions.

What does it cost to run?

About three cents of model time per appeal on Nebius Token Factory. Routing simple steps to Nemotron Nano and Super keeps it roughly 30% cheaper than sending everything to Ultra.

What happens to my documents?

They are processed in memory for your request and never written to disk on our server. Your cases are saved only in your own browser.

What if my appeal is denied again?

Most plans then allow an external review by an independent organization whose decision binds the insurer. Your denial letter lists the deadline — usually four months.
