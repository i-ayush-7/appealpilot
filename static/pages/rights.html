Know your rights

Plain-language guides to the six most common denial types, and the rules insurers have to follow.

Your baseline rights

You can appeal almost any denial — usually twice.

1

Internal appeal

Most plans give you 180 days from the denial notice to ask the insurer to reconsider.

2

External review

If they say no again, an independent reviewer can overrule them. Their decision binds the plan.

3

Your claim file

You can request every document and criterion used to deny you, free of charge.

General information, not legal advice. Deadlines and rights vary by plan type and state — your denial letter lists the ones that apply to you.
